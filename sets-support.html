Support — Sets

Last updated 7 September 2026

Email porcelain2015@gmail.com with any problem, question or request. Include your iOS version and what you were doing when it happened, and you'll get a reply.

Common questions

How do I correct a set I logged wrong?
Swipe left on it in the Today list and delete it, then log it again.

Where is my data stored?
On your device only. Sets has no account, no server and no network connection — see the privacy policy.

Will my data sync between devices?
No. Sets stores everything locally, which is what keeps it private and fast. Your data is included in your normal iPhone backup.

Is there a subscription?
No. Sets is a one-time purchase with no subscription and no in-app purchases.

Can I log bodyweight exercises?
Yes. Exercises like Pull-up record reps only — no weight field appears, and none is shown in your history.

Requesting a feature

Sets is deliberately small, and most of what it doesn't do is on purpose. That said, if something is missing that would make logging faster, say so — that's the kind of request that gets built.
