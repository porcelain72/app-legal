Support — Catch

Last updated 9 September 2026

Catch logs a fish — species, size, where, and the conditions at the time — and keeps it on your phone. Questions, problems and feature requests all go to porcelain2015@gmail.com.

Common questions

How do I log a catch?
Tap Log a Catch, pick a species, tap Save. The time, the spot, the weather and the moon phase are filled in for you. Weight and length are optional — open them only if you want them.

Why is there no weight on some of my catches?
Because you didn't record one, and that's a normal thing to do. Catch shows nothing at all rather than a zero, so a catch you didn't weigh still reads as a proper record.

How do I correct or delete a catch?
Tap it in the Catches list to open and edit it, or swipe left on the row to delete. Anything you can create, you can fix.

Can anyone else see where I fish?
No. Your positions are stored on your phone and there is no account, no feed and no server. Exports and shared catches leave positions out unless you switch them on for that one action, and that switch resets to off every time. See the privacy policy.

My catch says "Conditions will fill in when you're back online".
That's expected. The catch saved completely — the weather just hasn't been looked up yet because you had no signal. Open the app again once you're back in coverage and it fills itself in. You don't need to do anything.

Can I stop the app going online at all?
Yes. Turn off Record conditions in Settings and Catch makes no network requests whatsoever. New catches save without weather; moon phase still works, because it's worked out on your device from the date.

My species isn't in the list.
Search for it and choose Use "…" as a species — it's saved as typed and appears in your recents afterwards. The bundled list covers freshwater species for UK & Ireland and North America; the globe button in the species picker switches region, or shows everything.

Does it do sea fishing?
Not yet. Catch is built for freshwater. Sea angling needs tide data, which is a real external dependency, and doing it badly would be worse than not doing it — so it's honestly absent rather than half-there.

Can I use kilograms instead of pounds?
Yes — Settings → Units. It changes every catch you've already logged, both ways, with nothing lost. Sizes are stored independently of the units you read them in.

Will my catches sync between devices?
Not in this version. Catch stores everything locally, which is what keeps it private and fast. Your data is included in your normal iPhone backup, and you can export the whole log as CSV from Settings.

Is there a subscription?
No. Catch is a one-time purchase with no subscription, no in-app purchases and no ads.

Requesting a feature

Catch is deliberately small, and most of what it doesn't do is on purpose — no feed, no gear shop, no AI fish identification. That said, sea fishing with tides and syncing between devices are both genuinely on the list rather than ruled out. If something is missing that would make logging faster or more accurate at the waterside, say so.
