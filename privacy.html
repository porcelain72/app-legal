Privacy Policy — Objectify

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Objectify does not collect your data. It has no servers, no user accounts, no analytics, and no third-party tracking of any kind. Your reports, photographs and notes are files on your own device, and they stay there unless you choose to move them.

The rest of this policy explains the detail, including the two places where information does leave your device and what that involves.

Your reports stay on your device

Every Objectify report is an ordinary file in your own storage — on your Mac, your iPhone or iPad, or in your own iCloud Drive. Objectify reads and writes those files directly. There is no cloud service behind the app, nothing is uploaded to us, and we have no ability to see, retrieve or recover your reports.

This is a deliberate design decision. A condition report is professional evidence, often requested years after the work it records. It should belong to the person who made it.

iCloud

If you enable iCloud, Objectify syncs your reports, templates and settings between your own devices using Apple's iCloud service and your own iCloud account. That data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, not by us. We have no access to your iCloud account or its contents.

iCloud is optional. Objectify works fully without it.

Photographs and the camera

Objectify can add photographs to a report in two ways:

  • From your photo library. Objectify uses Apple's system photo picker, which hands the app only the specific images you select. Objectify never requests access to your photo library as a whole and cannot browse it.
  • From the camera. If you take a photograph inside Objectify, the app asks your permission the first time. The image is written straight into your report.

Location

If you use Objectify's location features, the app asks your permission and then uses your device's location to pre-fill a report or project location and to help you find nearby places.

This is one of the two places information leaves your device. To turn a coordinate into a readable address, and to search for nearby places by name, Objectify uses Apple's Maps services. Your coordinate or search text is sent to Apple, handled under Apple's Privacy Policy, and is not sent to us or to anyone else. This happens only while you are actively using the location picker — Objectify does not track your location in the background.

Location is optional. If you decline permission, every other part of Objectify continues to work, and you can still type a location by hand.

What travels inside a report you export

This section matters if you send reports to clients, registrars or institutions, because it concerns information that leaves your control when you share a file.

By default, an exported report carries:

  • The photographs' original metadata (EXIF), which may include the camera used, the date and time, and — if your camera recorded it — the coordinates where the photograph was taken.
  • The report's own recorded location, including latitude and longitude if one was set.

Both defaults exist because a condition report is evidence, and stripping provenance from evidence is usually the wrong thing to do. Both are also optional. Objectify's export options include Strip EXIF metadata and Redact location, which remove that information from the exported copy while leaving your original report untouched.

Once you send an exported file to someone else, what happens to it is outside Objectify's control and outside ours.

Subscriptions and payment

Objectify's subscription is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the purchase and handles all payment information. We never see, receive or store your payment details, and we do not receive your name, email address or Apple Account identity from Apple.

Objectify checks with the App Store whether your subscription is active. That check goes to Apple, not to us.

What Objectify does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting of any kind.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No third-party software libraries. Objectify is built entirely from Apple's own frameworks and our own code.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no profile held by us.
  • No data sold, shared or disclosed to anyone, because none is collected.

Information you enter about yourself

Objectify lets you record an examiner profile — your name, institution, contact details, and optionally a logo — so that it can appear on the reports you produce. This is stored on your device with your other settings, syncs through your own iCloud if you enable it, and is included in reports you export because that is its purpose. It is never transmitted to us.

Children

Objectify is a professional tool for conservators and collections staff. It is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
