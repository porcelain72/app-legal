Privacy Policy — Sets

Last updated 7 September 2026

Sets collects no data about you. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network connection of any kind. Nothing you log ever leaves your device.

What Sets stores

Sets stores the workout sets you log — the exercise name, weight, repetitions and the date — on your device, and nowhere else. This information is never transmitted anywhere, because the app contains no networking code at all.

What Sets does not do

  • No account or sign-in, so there is nothing to identify you by.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party SDKs or services of any kind.
  • No data is sold or shared, because none is collected.

Backups

If you have iPhone backups switched on, your Sets data is included in your own device backup, the same as any other app's. That backup is between you, your device and Apple; the developer of Sets has no access to it and receives nothing from it.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. There is no server-side copy to request the deletion of, and no account to close.

Children

Sets is suitable for general audiences and collects nothing from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Sets ever collects anything, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the app's release notes.

Contact

Sets is a one-time purchase with no subscription and no in-app purchases.
